Privacy policy

Eightlap keeps your plans and journal on your phone. There is no account.

What is stored, and where

Everything you enter — race plans, journal entries, times, notes and settings — is stored in a database on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we run no server that could receive it.

What is not collected

No name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or health data. No analytics of how you use the app, no advertising in it and no tracking. Eightlap does not use HealthKit.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Eightlap Pro is sold through the App Store. To process purchases and restore them, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that handles in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt and transaction details from Apple, an anonymous app user ID that it generates, and the technical data that comes with any network request, such as your IP address, device model, system version, language and country. It does not receive your plans, your journal or anything else you enter. We cannot tell who you are from this, and RevenueCat does not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites. RevenueCat's own policy, at revenuecat.com/privacy, describes how it handles that data.

Apple Ads attribution

Whether or not you came from an ad, the app asks Apple for an attribution token, a code that says nothing about who you are, and passes it to RevenueCat once per installation, together with the anonymous app user ID. If you installed Eightlap after tapping one of our ads in the App Store (Apple Ads), RevenueCat uses the token to ask Apple which campaign, ad group and keyword the ad belonged to, and keeps that with the same ID, so that we can see which ads lead to subscriptions. If you did not come from an ad, Apple reports no campaign. Eightlap does not read your advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not show the tracking-permission prompt, because nothing is combined with data from other companies' apps or websites. RevenueCat's policy, mentioned above, covers this data too.

Network access

Apart from the purchase processing and the ad attribution described above, and Apple's own App Store traffic when you buy or restore, the app makes no network requests.

What leaves the device, and only when you send it

A CSV export of your journal leaves the device only when you choose to share or save it, and goes only where you send it.

Children

Eightlap is a training tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Deleting an entry deletes it. Settings → Erase everything removes every plan and entry. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone, permanently, so export your journal first if you want to keep it. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat follow their own policies.
