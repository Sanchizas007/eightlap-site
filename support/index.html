Common questions

The plan's times do not match how I run

The plan starts from how race time usually divides. Open Weak spots on the Plan tab and move the sliders for the stations and the running where you are slower or faster than average: the plan re-balances itself and the total stays what you set.

Why does the forecast differ from my target?

The forecast uses your own results from the last 90 days: the best two for each piece. A piece you timed fresh is slowed to race pace, and anything you have not timed is filled in from the shape of the course. The more of the course you have logged, the firmer it gets.

How do I log several stations at once?

Journal tab, plus, pick Training, then tap each station you timed. Every one gets its own row and its own time. For a full run-through choose Simulation or Race and fill in the finish time and, if you like, every split.

Where did my older journal entries go?

They are still on your phone. The free version shows the latest ten, and the rest return the moment Pro is unlocked. Nothing is ever deleted for you.

I bought Pro and it is not showing

Settings → Unlock Pro → Restore purchase, signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with. Pro is a one-time purchase, so restoring it is free.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Eightlap's data is part of your iPhone backup, so restoring the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup brings it across. You can also export your journal as CSV from Settings and keep the file.

Does it work for doubles and relay?

Yes. Pick Doubles or Relay when you create the plan. The weights follow the rulebook for that format and division, and the time is divided with the shared work in mind.
